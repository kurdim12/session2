Who We Are

About IEEE UOP

The IEEE UOP Student Branch is one of Jordan's most active student branches, dedicated to fostering technical excellence, innovation, and professional development.

⚙

Our Mission

To inspire and empower students by providing access to IEEE resources, networking opportunities, and hands-on technical experiences.

💡

Our Vision

To be the leading student branch in the region, shaping the next generation of engineers and technology leaders.

🌐

Our Values

Integrity, innovation, collaboration, and commitment to advancing technology for the betterment of humanity.
